1. STUDY OVERVIEW

Study title: Everyday Wellbeing Sensing Study (example)
Principal Investigator: Dr. Jane Smith, Example University
Study contact: study-team@example.org
Duration: 4 weeks of passive data collection per participant

Purpose: To understand how everyday behaviors such as physical activity, sleep,
phone use and stress relate to self-reported mood and wellbeing. Participants
wear a Garmin smartwatch and install a study app on their iPhone. Both devices
collect data passively in the background, and participants answer short
surveys (EMAs) on their phone a few times a day.


2. ELIGIBILITY

- Adults aged 18 or older
- Own an iPhone running a recent version of iOS
- Willing to wear a smartwatch during the day and, if comfortable, at night
- Able to charge the watch and phone regularly


3. DEVICES

3.1 Garmin smartwatch
Participants receive a Garmin watch that is paired with the Garmin Connect app
and linked to the study platform. The watch is worn on the non-dominant wrist,
above the wrist bone, snug but comfortable.

3.2 Smartphone app
Participants install the study app on their own iPhone. The app runs in the
background and uploads data when the phone has an internet connection. It
also delivers EMA surveys as notifications.


4. DATA COLLECTED

4.1 From the Garmin watch
- Heart rate (HR): beats per minute, sampled every few seconds while worn.
  Each sample has a status such as VALID or SEARCHING (the sensor is still
  locking on to a reading).
- Beat-to-beat intervals (IBI / BBI): the time in milliseconds between
  consecutive heartbeats. Used to estimate heart rate variability and stress.
- Steps: step counts per interval and the running daily total.
- Stress: Garmin's stress score (0-100) derived from heart rate variability
  while the participant is inactive. Scores of 0-25 are rest, 26-50 low,
  51-75 medium and 76-100 high stress.
- Body Battery: Garmin's estimate of energy reserves (5-100), which drains
  with activity and stress and recharges with rest and sleep.

4.2 From the iPhone
- Activity: the phone's motion classification (stationary, walking, running,
  automotive, cycling) with a confidence level.
- Steps and distance: steps, distance and floors climbed recorded by the phone.
- Battery: battery level and charging state.
- Screen brightness: the screen brightness level from 0 to 1.
- Lock / unlock events: when the phone is locked and unlocked, used to
  estimate how often and how long the phone is used.
- App usage: which app is in the foreground and when it is opened and closed.
- Call log metadata: the time, type (for example dialing, incoming,
  connected, ended) and duration of calls. Phone numbers, contact names and
  call content are NOT collected.
- Wi-Fi: whether the phone is connected to Wi-Fi. Network identifiers are
  hashed or removed before analysis.

4.3 Surveys (EMA)
Participants receive 3 short surveys per day (morning, afternoon and evening)
at random times within fixed windows. Each survey takes about 1-2 minutes and
asks about current mood, stress, energy, social interaction and sleep quality
(morning survey only). A survey expires 60 minutes after it is sent.


5. PARTICIPANT RESPONSIBILITIES

- Wear the watch as much as possible, ideally at least 12 hours per day.
- Charge the watch every 3-5 days. Charging while showering or at a desk
  reduces time without data.
- Keep the phone app installed and do not force-close it.
- Keep Bluetooth on so the watch can sync with the phone.
- Answer as many EMA surveys as possible. The target is at least 70%.


6. DATA QUALITY AND COMPLIANCE CHECKS

The study team reviews each participant's data a few times a week:
- Watch worn time vs. watch on time: if the watch is on but worn time is low,
  the participant may not be wearing it. The team sends a friendly reminder.
- Data gaps: no watch data for more than 24 hours usually means the watch
  needs charging or has not synced. No phone data usually means the app was
  closed or the phone was off.
- EMA compliance: if a participant answers fewer than half of their surveys
  in a week, the team checks in to see whether notification settings or
  survey timing need adjusting.


7. TROUBLESHOOTING

- Watch not syncing: open the Garmin Connect app, check Bluetooth is on and
  pull down to refresh.
- App not collecting data: open the study app once so it can resume, and
  check that Location, Motion & Fitness and Background App Refresh
  permissions are enabled.
- Not receiving surveys: check that notifications for the study app are
  allowed and that Focus / Do Not Disturb is not blocking them.


8. PRIVACY

Data is stored on secure servers and linked to a study ID (for example
test004), not to the participant's name. Only the study team can access
identifiable information. Participants can pause data collection or withdraw
from the study at any time by contacting study-team@example.org.


9. COMPENSATION

Participants receive $10 per week of participation, plus a $20 bonus for
completing at least 70% of EMA surveys over the full study.
